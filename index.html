Mylly Rules (Nine Men's Morris)

Objective

Reduce your opponent to 2 pieces or block all their moves.

Phase 1 - Placement

  • Players alternate placing their 9 pieces on empty positions
  • Form a "mill" (3 pieces in a row) to remove an opponent's piece
  • Pieces in a mill cannot be removed unless no other option exists

Phase 2 - Movement

  • After all pieces are placed, move one piece to an adjacent empty position
  • Form a mill to remove an opponent's piece
  • You can repeatedly open and close the same mill

Phase 3 - Flying

  • When reduced to 3 pieces, you can move to ANY empty position

Connect Four Rules

Objective

Be the first to get four of your pieces in a row.

How to Play

  • Players take turns dropping pieces into columns
  • Pieces fall to the lowest available space in the column
  • Connect four pieces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win
  • If the board fills up with no winner, it's a draw
